# Doctor Cards are private to their Member

In MVP1 every Doctor Card has exactly one owning Member and no one else can see it — not even family. A shared family list and a household-sharing model were both considered; per-Member ownership is the simplest model and extends cleanly to friends and the public later. Household sharing is the expected v2 addition if managing a relative's doctors turns out to matter.
